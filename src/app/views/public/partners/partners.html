Onboard

Modern onboarding and implementation platform that turns launch plans into repeatable, scalable workflows

What Onboard Does

Onboard centralizes everything involved in customer onboarding and implementation into one shared workspace. The platform generates dynamic launch plans from your CRM data, assigns tasks to the right stakeholders, and automates reminders, status updates, and escalations. Customers get a secure portal with their plan, owners, and timelines, while your internal teams get clear visibility into every implementation in flight. The result is faster time-to-value, fewer surprises, and a consistent onboarding experience that scales.

How This Partnership Works

  • When we redesign your onboarding process and time-to-value targets, Onboard becomes the system where those playbooks live, with standardized launch plans, milestones, and automation
  • When we optimize your Professional Services organization, Onboard structures implementations into clear phases and responsibilities, improving predictability, utilization, and margin
  • When we architect your post-sale organization, Onboard provides the shared workspace for CS, Implementation, and Account Management so every team stays aligned on customer progress and launch risk
